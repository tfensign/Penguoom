PENGUØØM

The Emperor's Guard has locked down ten sectors of the Ice Fortress — polar bears, armored walruses, swarms of vicious stoats, and diving snow owls stand between you and the throne. Every sector you clear turns colder and meaner: walruses are slow but armored like tanks and hit far harder than a bear, stoats are fast, fragile, and relentless, and owls stoop out of the air to strike before wheeling back up out of reach. Click to lock your mouse and begin. Press 1/2/3 to switch between the Fish Blaster, Harpoon Cannon, and Flamethrower.

How to Play

  • Move: WASD
  • Look: mouse (aim up/down matters - it's the difference between a body shot and a miss), or scroll to turn
  • Fire: click and hold
  • Switch weapon: 1 (Fish Blaster), 2 (Harpoon Cannon), 3 (Flamethrower)
  • Pause: P, or the Pause button
  • Mute: M, or the music/SFX icons
  • Home icon: back to this screen to change difficulty - your run pauses first and asks you to confirm
  • Move: the joystick, bottom-left
  • Look/aim: drag anywhere on the screen
  • Fire: tap; hold for the Flamethrower
  • Switch weapon: tap a weapon icon, bottom-right
  • Pause/Music/SFX/Sectors/Home: the button column, top-left

Goal: fight through all ten icy sectors. Clearing a sector unlocks it for direct selection later (per difficulty) via Sectors.
