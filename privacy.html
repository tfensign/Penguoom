PENGUOOM - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

PENGUOOM is a single-player game with no account system, no server backend, and no advertising or analytics of any kind. This page explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your data.

What the app stores

The app saves a small amount of data using your browser's/device's local storage, entirely on your own device:

  • Your chosen difficulty level
  • Which sectors you've cleared, per difficulty
  • Your best scores/run stats
  • Whether music and sound effects are muted

None of this ever leaves your device. There is no server it could be sent to - the app has no backend at all.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal information.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
  • It does not require or use an account, login, or network connection to play.
  • It does not share data with any third party, because it does not collect any to share.

Permissions

The app may ask for microphone-free audio playback permission (to play its sound effects/music) and, on some platforms, motion/pointer permissions purely to read joystick and look-around input. None of these are used to identify or track you.

Children's privacy

Since the app collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the project's GitHub repository.
